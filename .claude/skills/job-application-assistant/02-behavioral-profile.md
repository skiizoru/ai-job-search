## Overview
A self-directed, quality-focused professional who combines meticulous analytical rigour with genuine collaborative instincts. Thrives in complex, long-horizon work where defensibility of decisions matters. Brings composure and credibility in high-stakes settings (expert witness, elected member briefings, Environment Court). Proactively innovates — building AI tools and introducing process improvements without being asked — while remaining a reliable, deadline-meeting operator in structured statutory environments.

## Strongest Behaviors

- **Synthesis under complexity:** Reads large volumes of technical material and produces clear, defensible positions. Comfort with ambiguity, but drives toward resolution.
- **Composure in adversarial settings:** Expert witness experience (three weeks before four Independent Commissioners; Environment Court mediation) demonstrates calm, professional advocacy under scrutiny.
- **Proactive initiative:** Introduced AI workflows and administrative process improvements independently — without being directed to — at multiple career stages.
- **Collaborative leadership:** Coordinates multi-disciplinary teams (8–10 specialists on plan changes) and mentors junior planners. Builds strong working relationships with applicants, iwi, elected members, and consultants.
- **Long-horizon commitment:** Led PC78 from 2021 to 2025 — a four-year end-to-end statutory process — with consistent quality throughout.

## How You Work Best
- Work that has intellectual depth and rewards careful analysis
- Environments with clear frameworks and rules (statutory, regulatory, or research-based)
- Collaborative but autonomous — trusted to manage complex work independently, with access to specialist input when needed
- Roles where quality of output matters more than volume of throughput
- Settings that value credibility and defensibility of decisions (hearings, policy, research)
- Coaching and knowledge-sharing opportunities alongside substantive work

- **Speed vs. thoroughness tension:** Strong preference for thorough analysis. Frame as: "I deliver quality under tight statutory timeframes — 100% compliance record — and know when to make a call vs. when to go deeper."
- **International equivalencies:** NZ-specific expertise (RMA, AUP, NZPI) needs active translation for international roles. Frame as: direct equivalents exist in every common law jurisdiction; planning frameworks are structurally similar and learnable.
