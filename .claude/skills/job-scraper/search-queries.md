# Search Queries for Job Scraper

## Target Markets
- **United Kingdom** — local and central government, urban policy think tanks, planning consultancy
- **Australia** — state/local government, planning consultancy, spatial technology
- **Singapore** — central government, urban planning agencies, GovTech
- **Canada** — federal/provincial/municipal government, planning consultancy
- **Nordics** — local/central government, think tanks, spatial technology
- **Remote** — global remote roles in policy, research, or GovTech

---

## Search Sites by Market

### United Kingdom
- **LinkedIn** (`linkedin.com/jobs`) — primary for all UK roles
- **Reed** (`reed.co.uk`) — broad UK job board with strong public sector coverage
- **Totaljobs** (`totaljobs.com`) — major UK job board
- **Civil Service Jobs** (`civilservicejobs.service.gov.uk`) — central government roles
- **Guardian Jobs** (`jobs.theguardian.com`) — public sector, policy, research, think tanks
- **RTPI Jobs** (`rtpi.org.uk/find-your-rtpi/jobs/`) — Royal Town Planning Institute job board (planning-specific)
- **LGJobs** (`lgjobs.com`) — local government jobs

### Australia
- **LinkedIn** — primary
- **Seek** (`seek.com.au`) — largest Australian job board
- **APSJobs** (`jobs.apsjobs.gov.au`) — Australian Public Service (federal government)
- **PIA Career Hub** (`planning.org.au/careerhub`) — Planning Institute Australia job board (planning-specific)
- **Indeed Australia** (`au.indeed.com`)

### Singapore
- **LinkedIn** — primary
- **MyCareersFuture** (`mycareersfuture.gov.sg`) — Singapore government job portal (includes public sector, statutory boards)
- **JobStreet Singapore** (`jobstreet.com.sg`)
- **Indeed Singapore** (`sg.indeed.com`)

### Canada
- **LinkedIn** — primary
- **Government of Canada Jobs** (`jobs.gc.ca`) — federal public service
- **Indeed Canada** (`ca.indeed.com`)
- **CIP/ICU Jobs** (`cip-icu.ca/resources/jobs`) — Canadian Institute of Planners job board (planning-specific)
- **WorkBC / provincial portals** — for provincial government roles (BC, Ontario, etc.)

### Nordics
- **LinkedIn** — primary (filter by Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland)
- **Finn.no** (`finn.no/jobb`) — Norway
- **Jobindex** (`jobindex.dk`) — Denmark
- **Arbetsförmedlingen / LinkedIn** — Sweden
- **TE-palvelut** (`te-palvelut.fi`) — Finland (also use LinkedIn for English-language roles)
- **Graduateland** (`graduateland.com`) — international roles in the Nordics

### Remote (global)
- **LinkedIn** — use remote filter
- **WeWorkRemotely** (`weworkremotely.com`) — remote jobs in tech, policy, research
- **Remote.co** (`remote.co/remote-jobs`) — curated remote roles
- **Remotive** (`remotive.com`) — remote roles in tech and adjacent fields

## Location Filter

When evaluating search results, verify the location is within scope:

- **United Kingdom** (any region): PASS — open to full UK
- **Australia** (any state): PASS — open to full Australia
- **Singapore**: PASS
- **Canada** (any province): PASS — open to full Canada
- **Nordics** (Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland): PASS
- **Remote / fully remote**: PASS
- **New Zealand / Auckland**: PASS — current base, acceptable if good opportunity
- **United States**: DISCUSS — not a stated priority; discuss with user if strong fit
- **Other locations**: DISCUSS

---

## Date Filter

Only include jobs posted within the last 14 days, or with an application deadline that has not yet passed. If a posting date cannot be determined, include it but flag as "date unknown".

---

## International Terminology Notes

When searching and reading postings, treat these as equivalent to NZ experience:

| NZ Term | International Equivalent to Search |
|---------|-------------------------------------|
| Plan change | Local plan amendment, development plan review |
| Resource consent | Planning application, development management |
| Section 32 | Sustainability appraisal, evidence base |
| Auckland Unitary Plan | Local plan, development plan, district plan |
| Independent Commissioner | Planning inspector, hearing officer |
| RMA | Town and Country Planning Act (UK), EP&A Act (NSW), Planning Act |
| NZPI | RTPI (UK), PIA (Australia), CIP (Canada) |

When searching, use both NZ and international terms to maximise results.

## Adapting Queries

If the user specifies a focus area, select queries from the matching category and generate 2–3 custom queries for that focus. For example:
- `/scrape UK government` → Priority 1 + Priority 2 queries filtered to UK civil service and local government
- `/scrape remote research` → Priority 4 + remote filter queries
- `/scrape Australia GIS` → Priority 3 queries on seek.com.au + LinkedIn Australia
