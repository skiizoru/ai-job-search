\lettercontent{Planning research should connect directly to built outcomes. That is how I have approached the work throughout my career. I am now seeking to bring that approach to URA's Strategic Research team.}

\lettercontent{The most relevant example of this approach is the Plan Change 78 city centre workstream, where I led applied research from initial concept through to operative policy. Key contributions included:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Applied R\&D management:} Scoped and led multi-party research from initial concept through to implementation, coordinating technical specialists, consultants and agency stakeholders to statutory timeframes.
    \item \textbf{Piloting and test-bedding new approaches:} Designed built form and density scenarios as a live research methodology, iterating based on technical and stakeholder feedback before policy adoption.
    \item \textbf{Comparative international research:} Assessed planning approaches across New Zealand and international cities to identify transferable solutions and inform policy responses to legislative reform.
    \item \textbf{Decision-ready outputs:} Prepared research briefings, spatial analysis and presentations for elected members and senior decision-makers.
\end{itemize}

I am also actively developing GIS capability through Esri training, and I use AI research tools including Claude Code daily to accelerate analysis and synthesis workflows. In this role, I would bring the same applied approach: scoping research questions with real planning implications and ensuring findings translate into outcomes URA can act on.}

\lettercontent{URA's commitment to long-term, evidence-based planning, reflected in the Draft Master Plan 2025's focus on urban resilience and liveability and the Long-Term Plan Review's fifty-year strategic framework, represents an environment where research leads directly to real decisions. I take initiative in scoping and driving research projects from the ground up, and I am ready to relocate to Singapore to contribute to that work.}
