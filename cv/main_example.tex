\vspace{6pt}
\small{Senior urban policy planner with 10 years of experience at Auckland Council, spanning resource consent processing, complex plan change development, and statutory hearings. Specialist expertise in city centre planning and Auckland Unitary Plan policy frameworks, with a strong track record leading multi-disciplinary plan changes from initial concept through to operative, including acting as council witness before Independent Commissioners. Skilled at translating spatial analysis, technical modelling, and international planning precedent into clear, defensible policy positions. Brings an applied interest in generative AI and spatial technology, having built AI-powered tools using Claude Code and NotebookLM to support policy work, project management, and document synthesis.}
